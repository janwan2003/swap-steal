White elephant, on everyone’s phone

Start a gift swap, share one link, and everyone joins with a nickname. Swapsteal draws the turn order, tells each person when it’s their turn, enforces the steal rules and keeps a live board of who holds what.

  • Free
  • No sign-up
  • No app to install
  • In the room or on a video call

Also known as Yankee swap, Dirty Santa, Chinese gift exchange, Schrottwichteln or the steal game.

Start a swap

No account or email. Swaps are deleted after 60 days.

How it works

Share one link

Post it in the group chat or show the QR code at the party. Guests tap it and type a nickname. Nothing to install.

Draw the order

When everyone’s in, the host taps Start. Swapsteal draws a random order and each phone buzzes when it’s that person’s turn.

Open or steal

Open a new gift or steal one from the board. Steal chains, frozen gifts and the final swap are all tracked. At the end you get a recap to share.

The rules Swapsteal runs

  • On your turn, open a new gift or steal any opened gift.
  • If your gift is stolen, you open or steal next. The turn ends when someone opens a new gift.
  • A gift can be stolen only once per turn, so nobody steals straight back.
  • After the set number of steals a gift is frozen and stays where it is.
  • Final swap (optional): player 1 never got to steal, so they may swap once at the end.
  • Online mode: you never open your own gift unless it’s the last one left.
  • The host can undo the last move and play for anyone without a phone.
  • Put the board on a TV with one tap so the whole room can follow.

Questions

Does everyone need a phone?

No. The host can add people by name (grandma, kids, anyone without a phone) and take their turns for them. Everyone else follows on their own phone, or on the TV board.

How does it work with real wrapped gifts?

Choose “In the room”. On your turn you pick a gift from the pile, unwrap it, and type what it is, such as “Singing fish”. Steals happen on the phone, and you hand the gift over in the room.

Can a remote team play?

Yes. Choose “Online”. Everyone enters the gift they will send, with an optional hint shown on the wrapping and a link that appears once it’s opened. Share the board on your video call. At the end the recap shows who ends up with which gift, so each giver knows where to send theirs.

How many people can play?

Up to 100 players in one swap. Most office and family swaps have 6 to 30.

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no ads, no per-game fee. Swapsteal never handles money or buys gifts. Gifts are just descriptions.

What do you store?

The swap name, nicknames, gift descriptions and the moves. Nothing else. No email, no cookies, no tracking. Each swap is deleted automatically after 60 days, or right away by the host. See the privacy policy.
