How it works

Ready in about a minute. Nobody needs an account or an app.

Create a swap

Name it, pick in-the-room or online, and set the steal limit. You get a link and a QR code to share.

Guests join

Everyone opens the link on their own phone and types a nickname. When you start, Swapsteal draws a random turn order.

Open or steal

On your turn, open a new gift or steal one that's already open. At the end everyone gets a recap card of who ended up with what.

Everything the game needs, nothing it doesn't

The rules are enforced for you, so nobody has to keep score on a notepad.

Every guest plays on their own phone

Your phone shows when it's your turn and what you can open or steal.

Steal limits and frozen gifts

Choose 1 to 5 steals per gift, or no limit. A gift that hits the limit is frozen with its owner.

No immediate steal-back

A gift can only be stolen once per turn, so nobody can grab it straight back.

Final swap

Player 1 never got to steal, so they can get one last swap at the end. Turn it on or off.

In the room or online

In the room, the opener types what was inside. Online, each player registers a hidden gift with an optional hint and link.

TV board

Put the live board on a TV or share it on a video call so the whole room can follow the steals.

Recap card

When the swap ends, see who got what, the most-stolen gift and the longest steal chain.

Free

Questions

Which rule variations can I set?

You choose how many times a gift can be stolen before it freezes (1 to 5, or no limit) and whether player 1 gets a final swap at the end. A gift can never be stolen straight back in the same turn. If your gift is stolen, you open or steal next, just like at the table.

How many players can join?

Up to 100 players in one swap.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no paid plans and no sign-up.

Does it work for remote teams?

Yes. Pick "Online" when you create the swap. Each player registers their gift before the game, hidden until it is opened, with an optional hint and link. Run the game on a video call with the TV board shared on screen, and each giver sends their gift to whoever ends up with it.

What happens to our data?

Swapsteal stores only the swap name, nicknames, the gifts players type in and the moves. There are no cookies, analytics or ads. Every swap is deleted automatically 60 days after it was created, and the host can delete it sooner. Read the privacy policy.
