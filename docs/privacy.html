Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-22 · Swapsteal is published by Plainbyte.

Swapsteal is a gift-swap game you play in the browser. There is no account and no email address. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

What is stored

To run a swap, the server keeps only what players type in and what happens in the game:

  • the name of the swap;
  • the nicknames players join with;
  • gift descriptions, hints and links typed in by players;
  • the moves: turn order, which gift was opened or stolen, and by whom.

Everyone who has the swap's link can see this information, so please do not type anything into a nickname or gift you would not want the other players to read.

How you are recognised

When you join a swap, your browser saves a random token in its localStorage. It lets you return to the same swap on the same device and play your turns. It is not a cookie, it contains no personal information, and you can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

IP addresses

To stop one visitor from overloading the service, IP addresses are hashed and the hash is kept for one hour for rate limiting. IP addresses are not stored otherwise.

Usage counts

To know whether Swapsteal is used, the service keeps a few totals per day: how many swaps were created, started and finished, how many people joined, and how many players the started swaps had. These are plain totals with no names, gifts, IP addresses or anything that points to a person or a swap.

Where data is kept

Swapsteal runs on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. Cloudflare is our hosting provider and processes requests to deliver the service. We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Every swap is deleted automatically 60 days after it was created. The host can delete a swap at any time before that, which removes its name, nicknames, gifts and moves.

Links to other sites

Gift links are typed in by players and open other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so do not include personal details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
